# Higgsfield shot list

Every image and video the site needs, with an ID the page code references and a prompt ready for Higgsfield. Real photography from the complex replaces any of these one for one: keep the ID, swap the file.

## Global style (prepend to every prompt)

> Photorealistic youth baseball, Virginia Beach, Virginia. Late afternoon golden light or overcast soft light. Low camera angle, 35mm or 85mm look, shallow depth of field. Infield dirt, green grass, chain-link fence, navy and white uniforms with no readable names, numbers or logos. No faces in sharp focus. No text anywhere in frame. Clean, warm, cinematic, editorial sports photography. Not illustrated, not HDR, no lens flare.

Tridents shots add: "navy and Columbia blue uniforms, dusk, stadium lights just coming on, sharper and moodier."

Champions shots add: "a player with a disability at bat or on the base paths with a teenage buddy beside them, joyful, inclusive, bright daylight."
